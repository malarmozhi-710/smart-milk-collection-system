Everything a dairy cooperative needs

Built for village collection centers — simple for operators, transparent for farmers.

🧪
Simulated Milk Analyzer
Auto-generates realistic fat readings (3.0–5.5%). Designed so a real analyzer device can plug in later via API.
💰
Automatic Payment
Rate = Fat% × ₹8 (configurable). Amount, totals and pending balances calculated instantly from the database.
👥
Farmer Management
Register, search, edit and manage farmer records with village, bank details and full history.
📊
Live Dashboard & Charts
KPI cards, daily trends, morning vs evening, farmer-wise and monthly charts — all from real data.
📄
Reports & Export
Daily, weekly, monthly, farmer-wise, payment and quality reports with CSV export and print.
🔒
Secure & Role-based
JWT auth, hashed passwords, role-based access for Admin and Farmer, parameterized SQL queries.
